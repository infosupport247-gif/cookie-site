Our Story

It started with a home oven, a butter-stained notebook, and one simple obsession: making a cookie so good it ruins all other cookies for you.

From a Home Kitchen
to Your Doorstep

In 2018, our founder Aditi was a home baker who couldn't find a cookie she truly loved in Mumbai. The grocery store ones were flat and artificial. The bakery ones were pretty but tasteless. So she did what any unreasonable person would — she spent six months perfecting a single chocolate chip cookie.

That cookie became two. Two became twelve. Friends begged for boxes. Colleagues placed orders at their desks. Within a year, Aditi's WhatsApp was a cookie hotline. Within two, Crumb & Co. had a kitchen, a team, and a simple mission: never compromise on the cookie.

Today, every cookie still starts with real butter — never margarine, never oil. Every batch is still mixed by hand. Every chocolate is still Belgian or single-origin. Because shortcuts show up in the taste, and we'd rather take the long way.
